## What was built
`src/ml/si_models/` (MODULE.md there has the API): three problems, two methods each, all scored on
held-out years with fixed seeds and bootstrap 95% intervals, n reported on every number.

| Problem | Method A | Method B | Scored against | App uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seasonal transitions (MODIS NDVI) | fixed cited threshold (0.1 bare-soil, provisional, global) | Savitzky-Golay + Pettitt change point | cited BRRI/BARC field-establishment windows | **B** — A is not assessed: the district-mean NDVI crosses 0.1 in only 3 of 200 windows; the Bangladesh threshold row is a GAP |
| Stressed seasons | z-score rule (limit 2, our choice) | Isolation Forest (cut-off at A's training flag rate) | 5 scored district-seasons = 2 independent events (see below) | **A** — B found no more of the listed events and flags more seasons outside them |
| Season types | percentile rule (top 20%, our choice) | k-means (k by silhouette; k = 2) | leave-one-year-out stability + held-out silhouette | **B** — silhouette 0.42 vs 0.25 (diff interval 0.14 to 0.20 above 0), stability no worse |

Headline numbers (full set: `docs/results/w3_metrics.json`, cards in `src/ml/si_models/cards/`):
- Stress, weather features 2001-2024 (360 district-seasons, 5 of them listed events): both methods flag
  all 5 listed district-seasons, which are **2 independent events** (Aug 2024 regional flood across 4
  districts; Sylhet haor flood 2017), flagged in both. A flags 108 of 355 other seasons (0.30, interval
  0.26-0.35), B 118 (0.33). With SMAP + NDVI (2015-2024, 140 rows) both still flag all 5 but flag
  63 of 135 (A, 0.47) and 91 of 135 (B, 0.67) other seasons: stated on the card. The district-season
  intervals treat the 4 Aug 2024 districts as independent, which they are not, so they overstate the evidence.
- Transitions, Method B: aman green-up median error 0 days outside the cited window (68% inside,
  n = 41); boro green-up median 11 days late (31% inside, n = 49) — the satellite sees the canopy,
  not transplanting, and 16-day composites cannot resolve less than about 16 days. Browning errors
  not assessed (no cited harvest window).
- Season types k = 2: wet-cool vs dry-hot (centres in the metrics JSON); stability 0.99.

  cut-off and the other districts' scores do not move. Seeds fixed (20261112).

- Model cards state n, intervals, limits and "needs local validation"; small-sample note on every card.
