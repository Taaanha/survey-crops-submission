# Handoff: W2 extra indicators (heavy-rain days, Tmin cold limits, POWER temperature vs BMD)

Branch `w2-extra-indicators`, cut from `main` after PR #41 was merged (merge commit ccb887f). Nothing committed or pushed.

## 1. What was done (plain English)
1. **Heavy-rain days** (`heavy_rain_days_bmd`): days in the season window with rain at or above 44 mm, beside the ETCCDI R20mm index (`extreme_rain_days_r20mm`, 20 mm). The `threshold` column says which limit each row used (44 or 20). The `dataset` text of every heavy-rain row says "cell-mean, not point rainfall; may undercount point extremes". Over 24 years: Sylhet 495 heavy days vs 1,405 R20mm days; Cumilla 254 vs 853; Feni 336 vs 999; Noakhali 327 vs 1,044; Brahmanbaria 287 vs 951.
2. **Cold limits**: 4 Tmin-only rows from `r04_cold_injury.csv` are added to the cold-night counts. Each limit is for the crop stage the source describes; the window is ours.
3. **POWER temperature vs BMD normals** (`docs/results/w2_temperature_check.md/.csv`): bias in degrees C per month and annual, four stations. Done, not dropped.
4. **GAP rows**: `load_crop_params_report()` returns the GAP rows of `crop_params.csv` as "not assessed" with their reason; `load_crop_params()` still returns numbers only. `python scripts/list_skipped_reference_rows.py` prints every row the strict loader skips.

## 2. The rows used (source_title, source_url, page)
- **Heavy rain:** `bmd_heavy_rain_min` = 44 mm per 24 h. Source title: "Heavy rain disrupts daily life in Dhaka (Financial Express, quoting BMD scale)". URL: https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/heavy-rain-disrupts-daily-life-in-dhaka. **Page: not recorded in the file.** A news report of the BMD scale, not a BMD document. The strict loader rejects this row (item name has no dots), so it is read straight from `r03_heavy_rain.csv`; the row's threshold_source says "raw row". I did not edit the file. If the teammate renames it to `climate.rain.heavy_day_mm`, W2 uses the loader with no code change.
- **Cold limits (Tmin):**
  - `boro_rice.cold.bamis_warning_tmin`, `mustard.cold.bamis_warning_tmin`, `potato.cold.bamis_warning_tmin`, each "below 10": BAMIS crop calendars (bamis.gov.bd/res/calendars/...), page not recorded, stage not captured. They give identical counts (the research notes say the same text appears across crops, so it may be a template).
  - `wheat.cold.ecocrop_tmin` 5: FAO Ecocrop Triticum aestivum, https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=211, "absolute minimum temperature for growth", generic.
  - Full titles, URLs and pages: `data/processed/indicators_thresholds.csv`.
- **Not used (63 r04 rows):** 57 are daily-mean, duration, lethal-minimum or temperature type not stated; 3 have a range or a note in the value (mustard frost seed kill, sesame regional, wheat BAMIS "below 15 (SUSPECT)"); 3 have no source link (Aman, maize, lentil BAMIS rows). The list prints when you run `python scripts/build_indicators.py`.

## 3. Findings (reported as found)
- **POWER Tmax reads cool** against the BMD normals: annual -0.4 (Comilla), -0.7 (Feni), -1.6 (Maijdi Court), -1.2 (Sylhet) C, and 38 of 48 station-months are below the normal, up to -2.8 C (Dec). Warming cannot explain it (the normals are 1981-2010, our data 2001-2024). Heat-stress days may be undercounted. POWER Tmin is close on the annual mean (at most 0.2 C) but single months miss by up to 1.5 C.
- **Period:** the temperature normals state their period (1981-2010); the instruction said unknown, which is true only for the rain normals. The report states both.
- **Heavy-rain counts are small** because IMERG is a cell mean: 44 mm days are about 30% of R20mm days.
