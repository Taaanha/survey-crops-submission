# Handoff: W5 map API, timeline, overlay, passport, scenario sliders (12 Nov 2026, branch w5-map)

## What was built
- `src/compute/map_layers/` (Layer 1, no AI) + MODULE.md; `tests/map_layers/` (40 tests) and `src/api/test_map_routes.py` (17).
- `src/api/map_routes.py`: five GET endpoints under `/api/v1/map/` (layers, outlines, values, scenario, passport), registered by two lines at the end of `app.py`. Proposal: `docs/contract_change_proposals.md` section 11 (`docs/api_contract.md` not edited).
- `web/app/map.html` (separate page; `web/app/index.html` untouched): layer switch with disabled not-assessed layers and their reason, year slider with greyed ticks, swipe compare of two layers, scenario sliders, Field Passport. It only calls the API.

## Scope answers
- **Outlines for all 64**: drawn. The file `data/reference/bgd_adm2_districts.geojson` is in the repo. Licence re-verified on 12 Nov against https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/BGD/ADM2/ : CC BY 3.0 IGO, source BBS / OCHA ROAP, boundary year 2020, 64 units (record in `data/reference/r13_boundary_licence.csv`). Shapes are the simplified geometry; attribution is shown in the page footer.
- **Layers**: NASADEM elevation (static) and slope (relative, labelled); GRACE-FO yearly mean (regional, shared cells, labelled; a year with no dates is empty, not 0); MODIS NDVI (2015-2024 only); SMAP yearly mean for the five live districts (full years only; 2015 starts 31 March so it has no value); W2 indicators for the five live districts by season window. Statuses are read from `docs/w1b_data_status.csv`. smap_64, ecostress_et, ecostress_lst, gedi_agbd, modis anomaly and hls_ndvi appear disabled with reasons.
- **Scenario**: Layer 1 `scenario.py`; at 0 % / +0 C it equals `crop_season_all_years` (tested). Label exactly "what-if on past seasons, not a forecast" (the only place those words appear; tested).
- **Passport**: CUM-ARF-01 and CUM-BRRI-02 (positions from the team's field record in `web/app/index.html`, not NASA data). NASADEM 30 m elevation and derived slope are drawn; the T3b flood-history layer is drawn only when `data/raw/opera_layers` exists (this machine has it; git does not), else "not assessed"; HLS, ECOSTRESS, MODIS 250 m and SMAP 9 km pixel grids are "not assessed" with reasons and their real pixel sizes.
- **Overlay compare**: done (swipe between two layers, web only).
- **Field-level vulnerability zones: NOT built** (lowest priority, cut). They need cited thresholds that are not in the repo; the passport says "not assessed".

## Decisions to review (ours, not cited)
- MODIS district-year value needs at least 12 of 23 usable composites; SMAP year needs 300 days.
- Scenario: rain amounts scaled on every day, temperature added to Tmax/Tmin/Tmean, humidity/wind/sun unchanged. Heat counts span the whole crop season though each limit is for one stage.
- Scenario crops: wheat, mustard, lentil, potato (FAO-56 model crops); sowing defaults to the start of the cited window.
- Kc values still lack a public page (existing open question); the scenario provenance says so.

## Fix made outside W5 folders
`district_grid/__init__.py` now imports lazily: the chain sat_layers > indicators > district_grid pulled shapely into the API, which `requirements-api.txt` does not install, so the deployed app would have failed to start.
