## 7. Mustard heat check (0.10)
Threshold row: `mustard.heat.flowering_tmax_low` = 25 degC (and `_high` = 29 degC), Tidy et al. (2025) Plant Stress 17:100957, p. ~1 (page unconfirmed), citing Angadi et al. 2000: "high-temperature stress can occur at >25C ... >29C leading to >50% reduction in seed yield". Used: onset end (25), variable `temp_max_c` (POWER daily max, degC, mean Nov-Feb about 26; no Kelvin), op `>`, 7-day flowering window, problem year = 3 or more days over the limit. Sowing in the rotation after Aman 15 Jun: 4 Nov every year (window 15 Oct-15 Nov, cited). GDD flowering day 35-52 (cited 37 d at mid-window), flowering window about 6-29 Dec. Tmax in that window: 22.3-26.5 C mean, highest day 23.9-27.8 C; days over 25 C: 18 of 24 years have one or more, 13 have 3 or more (= the 13 problem years); days over 29 C: none. So no unit error, no wrong variable, no wrong window. The result is genuine under the onset threshold; at the severe end (29) it would be 0 of 24. Recorded in PROJECT_STATE. Caveat: POWER Tmax is a coarse grid value; bias vs stations unchecked.
