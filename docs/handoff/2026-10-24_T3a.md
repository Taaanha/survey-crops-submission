- **Coverage (CMR, no downloads).** Collections: `OPERA_L3_DSWX-HLS_V1` and `OPERA_L3_DSWX-S1_V1` (short names read from CMR). **DSWx-S1 first date over both the Feni and the Cumilla 20 km boxes is 21 Aug 2024**; searching back to 1 Jan 2023 finds none earlier. Feni 75 dates, Cumilla 81 dates; stacks in `data/raw/opera_stacks/` (gitignored), manifests in `data/processed/field_profile_manifest_{feni,cumilla}.json` (53 KB, 117 KB) with granule IDs and WTR URLs per date.
