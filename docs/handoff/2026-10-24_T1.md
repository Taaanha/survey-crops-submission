# Handoff: T1 Rotation engine: 2026-10-24

## 1. What was done (plain English)
- **Built `src/compute/rotation/`**: it chains Kharif-2 Aman, then a rabi crop, using the existing risk calendar and Kc-weighted water balance through their public functions. It holds no threshold, stage length or water formula of its own.
- **Feasibility rule**: crop N+1 is sown on the later of (harvest of N + turnaround) and the start of its cited window; if that is after the window end, the rotation is infeasible that year. Infeasible years are counted apart from problem years.
- **Per rotation and year (2001-2024)**: feasible?, problem year?, irrigation mm (existing water balance), legume count. **Summary**: "problems in X of N years", irrigation in median and worst-20% years, legume count, all with provenance.
- **New crop table `data/reference/crops.csv`**: every filled cell was copied from an existing cited row and points at it (`*_src` columns); every blank is listed in `needs_source`. Nothing was guessed.
- **One small addition to `risk_calendar.py`**: public `season_row` (wraps `evaluate_season` + the water columns). `crop_calendar` is unchanged.
