# Handoff: W2 indicators, homogeneity check, satellite vs ground check

## 1. What was done (plain English)
- **Indicator module** `src/compute/indicators/` that turns a district's daily weather into a long table, per season window (kharif1, kharif2, rabi) and season year 2001-2024: heat-stress days, hot nights, cold nights (14 cited limits from `crop_thresholds.csv`), extreme-rain days (ETCCDI R20mm), Rx1day, dry spells (ETCCDI CDD), season ET0, rain minus ET0, and a SMAP root-zone z-score. Any district slug works; the driver takes slugs on the command line.
- **Not assessed, visible as such:** MODIS NDVI z-score, GRACE-FO trend, ECOSTRESS comparison (value empty, status "not assessed (... not downloaded yet, W1b)"). Never 0.
- **Trends with a homogeneity check** (`trend.py`): Theil-Sen + Kendall, then Pettitt on the detrended residuals.
- **Satellite vs ground** (`ground.py`): IMERG and POWER monthly rain 2001-2024 against BMD normals at four stations.
- **Scope:** the five live districts only, as decided on 11 Nov. Nothing waits for the W1b downloads.

## 2. How to run / check
```
python scripts/build_indicators.py          # tables in data/processed/indicators_*
python scripts/build_w2_reports.py          # docs/results/w2_ground_check.md, w2_homogeneity.md
python -m pytest tests/indicators -q
```
Output: `indicators_districts_2001_2024.csv` (7,245 rows, 2.2 MB: 6,985 ok, 215 "no SMAP data for this window" (before 31 Mar 2015), 45 not assessed), `indicators_trends_2001_2024.csv` (45 series), `indicators_thresholds.csv` (full citation of each limit), `indicators_explainers.csv` (one line per indicator). Row `threshold_source` holds item + URL; the full title and page are in `indicators_thresholds.csv`.

## 3. Findings you should know (reported as found)
1. **IMERG reads low against BMD normals**: annual -17% Feni, -18% Noakhali (Maijdi Court), -16% Sylhet, +8% Comilla. Its mean absolute monthly bias is larger than POWER's at all four stations (Comilla nearly equal). Biggest misses: July, -233 to -264 mm in Feni/Noakhali. Caveats: BMD period unknown, station vs 0.1 degree cell. This does **not** change the rain source: POWER rain has a step around 2014-15 (POWER/IMERG ratio breaks at p < 0.001 in all five districts, same as T9b), so its closeness can be offset luck. But rain totals and dry-spell counts should be described as estimates.
2. **The agreed trend rule has a weakness.** After a straight line is removed, POWER's jump is partly absorbed, so residual Pettitt called POWER rain a `trend` in 4 of 5 districts (1981-2025) and `step_change` only in Sylhet. `w2_homogeneity.md` therefore also shows Pettitt on the raw series and on POWER/IMERG (both detect the break). The rule is as specified, and IMERG/temperature/ET0 results use it unchanged; your call whether to change it (open question in PROJECT_STATE).
3. For our own series (IMERG rain, POWER Tmean, ET0; 45 tests at 0.05, ~2 expected by chance): 14 `trend`, 0 `step_change`. Kharif-2 mean temperature rises in all 5 districts (Kendall p < 0.02, +0.02 to +0.04 C per year); rabi/kharif-1 ET0 falls in several. Feni and Noakhali share one POWER cell, so those rows are not independent.
