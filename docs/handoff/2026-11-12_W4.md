# Handoff: W4 Rotation Strategy Exploration (12 Nov 2026, branch w4-strategies)

## What was built

`src/compute/strategies/` (Layer 1, no AI imports) + `tests/strategies/` (56 tests)
+ MODULE.md. Not wired into `app.py`: the endpoint is proposed as section 10 of
`docs/contract_change_proposals.md` (shared-file rule).

- **`build_strategies(district, current)`** (`core.py`): the farmer's CURRENT
  rotation is the required baseline (candidate id or a slots dict; a missing or
  unknown `current` raises ValueError naming the known ids); every other
  feasible candidate from the rotation engine is an alternative. Cumilla gives
  the baseline + 4 alternatives. Returns baseline, alternatives,
  crops_not_offered, priority_matrix, pest_rule, assumptions.
- **Indicators** (`indicators.py`), each `assessed` with cited measures or a
  `gap` with its reason, never 0:
  - Kc-weighted FAO-56 water use: straight from `evaluate_rotation` (never cut;
    existing tests untouched and passing).
  - Problem years out of 24 with a bootstrap range — same settings as W3
    (`SEED=20261112, N_BOOT=2000, LEVEL=0.95`), reimplemented with numpy only
    so Layer 1 never imports `src/ml`.
  - Bare-field days per 365-day year from OUR sowing-to-harvest windows,
    labelled "not satellite-observed".
  - Heavy-rain days: cited BMD row (44 mm, raw read as in W2) else ETCCDI
    R20mm; `which` says which one was used.
  - NASADEM slope tercile class (1..3 across the 64 districts in
    `nasadem_district_static.csv`): relative ranking only, breakpoints are our
    choice, reason text from `docs/w1b_data_status.csv`.
  - Erosion exposure = bare days x heavy-rain days x slope class, a relative
    index only.
  - Per-crop cited facts (`facts.py`): FAO-56 Table 22 root depth, residue and
    price rows via the strict loader; crop family, pest-cycle rule and forage
    read RAW with a source-URL check because the strict loader skips every row
    of `crop_family.csv` and `forage.csv` (unit/value format) — the provenance
    note says so. Pest-cycle break is labelled "needs local review". Prices
    are market prices (BDT/kg), never income per hectare (no yield model).
- **Five-heading card** (`cards.py`): Why suggested, Evidence, Benefits,
  Information gaps, Risks to check locally (always starts with the SAAO line).
  Deterministic templates; "predict"/"forecast" never appear (tested on the
  whole JSON).
- **Priority matrix** (`matrix.py`): water, soil health, low risk, income,
  with T2's honesty rules (min-max, gaps never scored, no_contrast flagged,
  ties listed).
- **crops_not_offered**: khesari, maize, sesame, chickpea etc. have reference
  rows but no cited duration/window/Kc chain in `crops.csv`; sesame's GAP rows
  in `new_crop_params.csv` are named in its reason.
