# Handoff: T3b field water profile (per-pixel flood metrics, layers, field_profile)

## 1. What was done (plain English)
- **Per-pixel flood events** (`events.py`): for each 30 m pixel, the number of flood events, first and last wet date, first dry date, a drain WINDOW and inundation days (min and max). Nothing is interpolated across cloudy dates; if the gap is too long the status says "too few observations" and there is no drain date. A pixel never observed reports "no observations", not zero events.
- **Two views.** Open water is the flood signal. Any water (adds inundated vegetation and partial water) is context only, because standing Aman paddy also looks like that.
- **Encoding changed** (`core.py`): 2 = open water, 1 = inundated vegetation / partial water, 0 = not water, 255 = no observation. T3a used 1 for all water. DSWx-S1 HAND-masked pixels follow the rule `hand_masked_as` (default not water).
- **Rules** are in `data/reference/field_profile_rules.csv`. They are PROJECT RULES, not from the OPERA spec (min 2 observations per event, drain window at most 12 days, 2 dry observations separate events).
- **Layers and answer** (`profile.py`, `hf.py`): `write_layers` saves GeoTIFF layers plus a manifest (outside git); `field_profile(lat, lon)` reads one pixel and returns metrics with provenance (datasets, granule IDs bounding the drain window). JRC Global Surface Water occurrence is included and labelled non-NASA. Outside the 20 km box: `covered: False`. `source='hf'` downloads a district's layers from Hugging Face first.
