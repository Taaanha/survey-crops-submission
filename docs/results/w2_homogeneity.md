# W2 homogeneity check: trends, level shifts, and why POWER rain is excluded

Generated by `scripts/build_w2_reports.py`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script.

**Method.** For each yearly series: Theil-Sen slope and Kendall tau test; then Pettitt's change-point test on the detrended residuals. `step_change` (residual Pettitt p < 0.05) means a level shift: it is **not shown as a trend**. `trend` means Kendall p < 0.05 and no step. 0.05 is a conventional level, our choice, not a requirement from a source. The Pettitt p-value is an approximation (Pettitt 1979), and the test finds one break at most.

**Reading these honestly.** 60 series were tested at the 0.05 level, so about 3 would show up by chance alone; 19 are marked `trend` and 0 `step_change`. Season Tmean and ET0 come from NASA POWER (about 0.5 degree), and Feni and Noakhali share one POWER cell, so their temperature and ET0 rows are not independent. We have not checked POWER temperature against an independent station record, so a clean result here shows no level shift within POWER, not that POWER is right.
