Generated by `scripts/build_w2_reports.py`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script.

Ground: Bangladesh climate normals 1981-2010, Table 3.1: Monthly normal maximum temperature (C) for different observatories of BMD (MET Norway report 08/2016, BMD observatory data), https://www.met.no/publikasjoner/met-report/met-report-2016/_/attachment/download/b50e3f06-4485-4f34-b642-0a1e86f12c7d:a483b63367b0f087f3bd9410e135a5e0837d1927/MET-report-08-2016.pdf, page 19. Model: NASA POWER daily `T2M_MAX` / `T2M_MIN` (AG community, about 0.5 degree cell at the district point), monthly means of 2001-2024 (every day of a month observed). Bias = POWER minus normal, in degrees C.

## Caveats (read before using the numbers)

- **Averaging period:** the normals file states Period 1981-2010 (a MET Norway / BMD report, not a BMD publication). Our POWER window is 2001-2024, so the two cover different years and part of any bias can be warming, not POWER error. The BMD rain normals, by contrast, state no period at all.
- A station is a point; POWER is a 0.5 degree cell (about 55 km), so elevation and coast effects inside the cell are averaged out. Feni and Noakhali share one POWER cell, so their POWER temperatures are identical.
- Four stations is a small check, not a validation of every district; Brahmanbaria has no station.

## Where POWER is worse

The five largest monthly misses: Feni TMAX Dec -2.8 C; Maijdi Court (Noakhali) TMAX Nov -2.7 C; Sylhet TMAX Dec -2.6 C; Sylhet TMAX Jan -2.6 C; Comilla TMAX Mar +2.5 C.
- **Tmax is the weaker variable**: mean absolute monthly bias 1.4 C against 0.7 C for Tmin, and POWER Tmax is below the normal in 38 of 48 station-months. A 1981-2010 normal compared with 2001-2024 data would make a warmer POWER more likely, so the cool Tmax is not explained by warming.
- A cool Tmax can undercount heat-stress days (days Tmax above a limit), most of all for limits close to the typical Tmax.
- Tmin is close on the annual mean (0.2 C at most), but single months miss by up to 1.5 C, which moves cold-night counts. Read both as estimates from NASA data for the area around the point, not station records.
