# rotation engine (T1)
Layer: 1 compute
Code: src/compute/rotation/core.py, src/compute/rotation/context.py, src/compute/rotation/crop_table.py
Purpose (2–3 sentences): Builds full-year rotations (Kharif-2 Aman, then a rabi crop, then Kharif-1) by chaining the existing per-crop risk calendar and Kc-weighted water balance. For each rotation and each past year (2001-2024) it says whether the rotation is feasible (harvest + turnaround is not after the next crop's sowing window), whether any assessed crop hit a problem, the irrigation need in mm and the number of legumes. A record of past seasons, not a forecast.
Public API (exact signatures + one-line description each):
- `candidate_rotations(district, context=None, include_infeasible=False)` : rotations from the crop table (each cited Aman seedbed date x each rabi crop with a cited duration, window and risk spec), pruned by a nominal-dates check.
- `evaluate_rotation(district, rotation, years=None, context=None)` : summary ("problems in X of N years", irrigation mm median and worst 20%, legume count, all with provenance) plus per-year details.
- `excluded_crops(context=None, district='cumilla')` : crop-table rows that cannot be chained yet and why (NEEDS SOURCE).
- `load_context(district, with_weather=True)` : the real context (crop table, risk-calendar specs, weather with ET0, water-balance inputs); cached.
- `load_crop_table(path=CROP_TABLE_PATH)` : data/reference/crops.csv as strings.
- Class `RotationContext` with fields crop_table, specs, weather, water, turnaround_days, weather_provenance, params, aman_duration_from : lets tests (and later tasks) hand in synthetic inputs.
Inputs (data files / other modules) and their provenance: public API of risk_calendar (`build_crop_specs`, `prepare_weather`, `calibrate`, `mean_climate`, `season_row`, `summarise`, `season_date`, `worst_mean`) and, through it, water_balance; `data/reference/crops.csv` (cited cells, each pointing at the existing reference row it came from); `common.measured`. One small addition was made to risk_calendar: `season_row` (a public wrapper of two functions it already had; `crop_calendar` is unchanged).
Outputs (shape + units + provenance fields): `{"district", "rotation", "summary", "years"}`. Every number is `{"value", "unit", "provenance": {dataset, source_url, retrieved, granule_or_file, note}}`; the summary also holds `problem_years_by_hazard` ({hazard: years}, added in T2 for flood safety) and each modelled slot of a year lists `hazards_hit`; year labels (`year`) identify rows and are not measurements. `retrieved` for weather is the data file's last-modified date (the repo stores no fetch date).
Reference tables used (data/reference/*.csv rows): crops.csv (new, wide; `*_src` columns point at crop_calendar.csv, crop_thresholds.csv, crop_params.csv rows and data/processed/kc_table.csv); everything risk_calendar already uses.
Tests (file names + what each proves): tests/rotation/test_rotation_acceptance.py (long Aman then mustard is infeasible; repeated runs identical; irrigation equals the existing water balance; "problems in X of N" equals a hand count; every number has provenance; per-year numbers equal the prebuilt risk calendar), tests/rotation/test_rotation_crop_table.py (every filled cell has a source, every blank is flagged NEEDS SOURCE).
Known limitations / honest caveats:
- Aman has no hazard or water model in the repo, so Aman's own risk (flood exposure, cold at flowering) is **not yet assessed**, and its problems and irrigation are not counted. It is NOT identical across candidates: the variety and duration are the same (BRRI dhan79, 135 days), but the seedbed date is 15 Jun in four candidates and 15 Jul in the boro-only one (`aman_rice@07-15>boro_rice`). So comparisons between Aman classes (and between a rotation with the 15 Jul Aman and one with the 15 Jun Aman) are incomplete: the ranking only reflects the rabi crop. Boro rice has no sourced hazard either, so its problem count is reported as "not assessed", never as zero.
- The Aman life cycle (135 days, BRRI dhan79) is counted from the seedbed date by default; the BRRI row does not say whether it starts at sowing or transplanting. `aman_duration_from='transplanting'` gives the later, more careful harvest.
- Turnaround (7 days) is the existing CropShift assumption in risk_calendar, not a cited value.
- Blank cells in crops.csv (NEEDS SOURCE): salt ECt and slope (FAO-29), (legume flags are now filled from cited pages; a blank would be listed in `legume_unknown_crops`), Kharif-1 crops (mung bean has no window or duration), potato maturity (its row is a PLACEHOLDER), photoperiod-sensitive Aman duration, per-region windows (the table holds the national BARC/BARI window).
- Kharif-1 is always fallow today. Three-crop chains (rabi then boro) are pruned by the nominal-dates check because the cited durations do not fit.
- Infeasible years are counted apart from problem years (`infeasible_years`, `problem_or_infeasible_years`).
