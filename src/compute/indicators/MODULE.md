# indicators (W2: heat, cold, rain, dry spells, evaporation, soil moisture, trends, ground check)
Layer: 1 compute
Code: src/compute/indicators/core.py, src/compute/indicators/thresholds.py, src/compute/indicators/trend.py, src/compute/indicators/ground.py, src/compute/indicators/build.py, src/compute/indicators/explain.py, src/compute/indicators/layer_status.py, src/compute/indicators/heavy_rain.py, src/compute/indicators/cold_limits.py
Purpose (2–3 sentences): Turns a district's daily weather into a long table of "understanding conditions" indicators per season window (2001-2024): heat-stress days, hot nights, cold nights (cited crop limits), extreme-rain days, dry spells, evaporation, a simple rain-minus-evaporation balance and a SMAP soil-moisture z-score. It also tests long-term trends with a level-shift check, and compares satellite rain with BMD station normals. Works for any district slug; MODIS, GRACE-FO and ECOSTRESS rows are written as "not assessed" until those downloads exist.
Public API (exact signatures + one-line description each):
- `window_series(series, season, year)` : daily values of one of our season windows, or None unless every day is present and not NaN.
- `window_days_observed(series, season, year)` : (days observed, days in the window).
- `count_beyond(values, limit, op)` : days strictly above (">") or below ("<") a limit (reuses risk_calendar.count_days).
- `r20mm_days(rain)` : ETCCDI R20mm, days with rain >= 20 mm.
- `rx1day(rain)` : largest one-day rain in the window (mm).
- `cdd(rain)` : ETCCDI CDD, longest run of days with rain < 1 mm (reuses agroclimate.dry_spell_length).
- `season_total(values)` : sum over the window.
- `climatic_balance(rain, et0)` : season rain minus season ET0 (mm); not the Kc-weighted crop balance.
- `leave_one_out_z(by_year, year, min_other=MIN_OTHER_YEARS)` : (z-score against the other years' mean and sample sd, number of other years); NaN with fewer than 5 other years.
- `classify_item(item)` : (kind, variable, op) for a crop_thresholds.csv item name, or None if we do not count it.
- `select_thresholds(kept)` : the countable cited limits from kept reference rows, repeated item+value removed.
- `load_thresholds()` : all cited heat / hot-night / cold limits from data/reference/crop_thresholds.csv (placeholder rows are already rejected by load_reference).
- `trend_status(years, values, alpha=ALPHA, unit='')` : Theil-Sen slope and Kendall p, then Pettitt on the detrended residuals; status trend / step_change / no_trend / not_enough_years plus a plain sentence.
- `monthly_mean_rain(daily, years)` : mean monthly rain total over the years, counting only fully observed months; returns mean_mm and n_years by month.
- `bias_table(satellite, normal)` : satellite minus station normal by month and annual: mm and percent of the normal.
- `build_district_table(district, weather, et0, smap, thresholds, years, seasons=SEASON_NAMES, layers=None, heavy_rain=None)` : the long table (columns in COLUMNS) for one district; `layers` may hold the status table, the MODIS table and this district's GRACE-FO series; `heavy_rain` is the cited heavy-rain row (None gives a not-assessed row).
- `row(district, year, season, indicator, value, unit, ds, n, status, threshold=np.nan, threshold_source='')` : one row of the long table.
- `lookup(table, key)` : (status, reason) of one layer in the W1b status table; a missing key raises KeyError.
- `smap_missing_status(table)` : status text for a district without a SMAP point file, from the `smap_64` row.
- `season_values(series, season, years)` : mean of the values dated inside each year's season window, NaN where none.
- `modis_row(district, season, table, modis_df)` : MODIS NDVI z-score row; status from the table, n = years present read from the data; fewer than 6 years or no data gives not assessed; value stays NaN.
- `grace_row(district, season, table, grace, years)` : GRACE-FO trend row (cm per year), always labelled regional (about 300 km, shared cells); slope only when the series is a trend, else NaN.
- `ecostress_row(district, season, table)` : ECOSTRESS comparison row; not assessed while the units are unresolved.
- `layer_rows(district, seasons, table=None, modis_df=None, grace=None, years=range(2001, 2025))` : the three satellite-layer rows per season, reading the status table (docs/w1b_data_status.csv) when none is passed.
- `heavy_rain_days(rain, limit)` : days with rain at or above a cited heavy-rain limit (the limit's own value counts).
- `load_heavy_rain_threshold(reference_dir=REFERENCE_DIR)` : the cited heavy-rain row as a dict with item, value, source_title, source_url, page and how it was read (`via`); `climate.rain.heavy_day_mm` via the strict loader if present, else the BMD row `bmd_heavy_rain_min` read from r03_heavy_rain.csv; None if no cited row.
- `parse_limit(value)` : a plain number or "below N" as a float; ranges and notes such as "(SUSPECT)" give None.
- `is_tmin_row(item, unit)` : True for a minimum-temperature limit; daily-mean, duration, lethal-minimum and type-not-stated rows are not.
- `select_cold_limits(table)` : (limits, rejected) from raw r04 rows: Tmin-only, cited, one plain number, item+value de-duplicated; each rejected row has a reason.
- `load_cold_limits(reference_dir=REFERENCE_DIR)` : the same for data/reference/r04_cold_injury.csv.
- `monthly_mean_temp(daily, years)` : mean monthly temperature (degC) over the years, counting only fully observed months.
- `temperature_normals(table, station, kind)` : a station's 12 monthly normals for 'tmax' or 'tmin' from reference rows; missing months are NaN.
- `temp_bias_table(model, normal)` : POWER minus station normal in degC by month; the annual row is the mean of the months.
- `family(indicator)` : the part of an indicator name before "__".
- `explain(indicator)` : the one-line plain-English explainer for an indicator.
Inputs (data files / other modules) and their provenance: docs/w1b_data_status.csv (via sat_layers.status: status and reason of the MODIS, GRACE-FO, ECOSTRESS and SMAP-64 layers), data/processed/modis_district_<year>.csv.gz and grace_district_monthly.csv (W1b); load_weather(district) (IMERG V07 Final Run rain + NASA POWER temperature); FAO-56 ET0 from water_balance.weather_table; data/processed/smap_<district>_2015_2026.csv (SMAP L4 root zone, SPL4SMGP.008); data/reference/crop_thresholds.csv (cited rows only); data/reference/bmd_normal_rainfall.csv (BMD monthly normals); ETCCDI index definitions http://etccdi.pacificclimate.org/list_27_indices.shtml.
Outputs (shape + units + provenance fields): data/processed/indicators_districts_2001_2024.csv (district, season_year, season, indicator, value, unit, threshold, threshold_source, dataset, source_url, n, status), indicators_trends_2001_2024.csv, indicators_thresholds.csv, indicators_explainers.csv; docs/results/w2_ground_check.md/.csv and w2_homogeneity.md.
Reference tables used (data/reference/*.csv rows): crop_thresholds.csv (14 cited rows: 7 Tmax heat, 2 hot-night Tmin, 5 cold Tmin), r04_cold_injury.csv (4 Tmin rows used: BAMIS below-10 warnings for Boro, mustard, potato; FAO Ecocrop wheat Tmin 5; the other 63 rows are listed as not used, with reasons), r03_heavy_rain.csv (BMD heavy rain 44 mm per 24 h; R20mm stays 20 mm from ETCCDI), bmd_normal_rainfall.csv (48 rows), bmd_normal_temperature.csv (4 stations x tmax/tmin x 12 months, period 1981-2010 as the file states).
Tests (file names + what each proves): tests/indicators/test_indicators_heavy_rain.py (44 mm counts 44.0, R20mm counted beside it with a different number, row read with source and no page, no cited row means None and a not-assessed row, never 0), test_indicators_cold.py (parse_limit and is_tmin_row cases, a table counted by hand with duplicates and rejects, the real file gives 4 limits), test_indicators_temp_ground.py (monthly means and bias in degC by hand; annual is a mean), tests/indicators/test_layer_status.py (fewer than 6 MODIS years is not assessed, years read from the data, table reasons carried, missing dataset or key, never 0, GRACE-FO slope by hand and regional label, a stale status table or indicator file fails), tests/indicators/test_indicators_core.py (window rule, strict counts, R20mm, Rx1day, CDD, totals, z-score by hand), test_indicators_trend.py (perfect line = trend, jump = step_change with the right year, constant, alternating, NaN, too few years), test_indicators_ground.py (monthly means and bias worked by hand), test_indicators_build.py (a fixture year counted by hand through the whole table; incomplete windows give NaN not 0; not-assessed rows; threshold selection and the real reference file).
Known limitations / honest caveats: Heavy-rain days use a limit from a news report quoting the BMD scale (no page, no BMD document); the strict loader rejects that row's item name (no dots), so it is read from the file and the row says so. IMERG is a 0.1 degree cell mean, so a point limit is reached less often: every heavy-rain row says "cell-mean, not point rainfall; may undercount point extremes". Cold limits from r04 are the 3 BAMIS below-10 warnings (stage not captured; the research notes say the same text appears across crops, so they may be a template) and one generic FAO Ecocrop value; all other r04 rows have a daily-mean, unstated or non-numeric temperature type and are not used. POWER Tmax reads 0.4-1.6 C cool against the BMD (MET Norway) normals (see w2_temperature_check.md). The MODIS NDVI z-score row has status and n but no season-level value yet (per-composite z-scores exist in sat_layers.loaders.ndvi_anomaly). The GRACE-FO trend is regional (about 300 km, 48 cells for 64 districts, irregular dates, one 370-day gap) and a trend is reported only when no level shift is found; the layer is 'partial' in the status table. ECOSTRESS is not assessed (units unresolved). Season windows (kharif1, kharif2, rabi) are our analysis windows, not a cited crop calendar, and each limit is for one crop stage, so a count is "days above the limit in our window", not "days of damage". R20mm, Rx1day and CDD are standard ETCCDI indices, not Bangladesh-specific limits (no sourced Bangladesh heavy-rain row exists yet). Rx1day and CDD are counted inside a season window, not per month or year as ETCCDI defines them. The trend rule (detrend, then Pettitt) can absorb a jump in a series into the fitted line, so the homogeneity report also shows Pettitt on the raw series and on POWER/IMERG. alpha = 0.05 is a conventional level, our choice. SMAP has only about ten usable years; always show n. IMERG reads low against BMD normals at 3 of 4 stations (see w2_ground_check.md).
